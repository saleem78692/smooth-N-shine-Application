Skin Concerns & Solutions

Every skin condition requires a precise clinical approach. Discover doctor-curated treatment regimens for active acne, stubborn pigmentation, premature aging, and dehydration.

Acne & Deep Acne Scars

Inflammatory comedones, cystic acne, and post-inflammatory ice-pick scars caused by excess sebum, bacteria, and delayed cellular turnover.

Recommended Clinical Regimen:

  • ✔ Salicylic & Azelaic Medical Peels
  • ✔ Carbon Laser Pore Decongestion
  • ✔ Face PRP & Micro-Needling Scar Revision
View Treatments

Pigmentation & Melasma

Hormonal discoloration, sun damage, and patchy melasma patches triggered by melanin overproduction in deeper dermal layers.

Recommended Clinical Regimen:

  • ✔ Q-Switch Nd:YAG Laser Pigmentation Toning
  • ✔ Yellow Peel & Glutathione Infusion
  • ✔ Korean Glass Skin Radiance Peel
View Treatments

Aging & Fine Lines

Gradual loss of natural collagen, elastin breakdown, crow's feet, smile lines, and loss of facial firmness over time.

Recommended Clinical Regimen:

  • ✔ Exosome & Stem-Cell Face PRP Therapy
  • ✔ Multi-Polar Radiofrequency Skin Tightening
  • ✔ 24K Peptide Anti-Aging Infusion
View Treatments

Dull, Tired & Dehydrated Skin

Pollution exposure, impaired lipid barrier, surface dead-skin build-up resulting in rough texture and lack of luminous glow.

Recommended Clinical Regimen:

  • ✔ 7-Step Korean Glass Skin Facial
  • ✔ Diamond Flake Microdermabrasion Polish
  • ✔ Hydra Medi-Facial with Hyaluronic Infusion
