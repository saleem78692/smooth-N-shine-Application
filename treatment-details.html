Front Laser Hair Removel (LHR)

Experience advanced Front Laser Hair Removal (LHR) in the comfort of your home. Our treatment uses clinically guided laser technology, customized by certified professionals according to your skin tone, hair type, and treatment area for smoother-looking skin and long-lasting hair reduction.

About The Treatment

Front Laser Hair Removal (LHR) is a professional laser-based treatment designed to reduce unwanted hair from the front body area. The treatment targets hair follicles using controlled laser energy, helping reduce visible hair growth over a series of sessions. At Smooth N Shine, the treatment is performed using advanced laser technology with settings selected according to your skin tone, hair type, and treatment area. With a personalized treatment plan and regular sessions, you can achieve smoother-looking skin with longer-lasting hair reduction.

The 7-Step Ritual

01

Stage 1:

Hair is trimmed, and the skin's surface is cooled.

02

Stage 2:

Laser targets pigmented cells, which heats up the hair follicle.

03

Stage 3:

Heat weakens follicle, damaging the palpilla, which is responsible for hair growth

04

Stage 4:

Damaged hair gradually falls out, and grows back finer or sparser.

05

Stage 4:

After a few series of sessions, the follicle is unable to regrow hair.

Before Treatment

  • Avoid waxing, threading, tweezing, or epilating the treatment area for at least 4 weeks before your laser session.
  • Shave the treatment area around 24 hours before your appointment, unless your clinician advises otherwise.
  • Keep the treatment area clean and avoid applying creams, oils, deodorant, or heavy products before your session.
  • Inform your clinician about any active skin irritation, cuts, infection, or recent tanning in the treatment area.

After Treatment

  • Keep the treated area clean and moisturized with a gentle, fragrance-free moisturizer.
  • Avoid hot showers, steam, sauna, swimming, and intense workouts for around 24–48 hours if the skin feels sensitive.
  • Avoid waxing, threading, tweezing, or plucking between laser sessions. Shaving is generally preferred.
  • Protect exposed treated skin from direct sunlight and use broad-spectrum SPF 30–50+ as recommended by your clinician.
  • Avoid harsh scrubs, exfoliants, and fragranced products until the treated skin has settled.

Laser Hair Removal Process

Experience a simple, comfortable and professionally guided journey towards smoother-looking, hair-free skin.

01
Step 01

Treatment

Your certified laser technician will determine the appropriate spot size and laser fluence based on your treatment area and skin type. Our advanced laser technology helps make your treatment comfortable, precise and efficient.

02
Step 02

Hair Sheds

Treated hairs generally begin to shed within around 10–14 days. You can allow the hairs to shed naturally or gently exfoliate the area using a body scrub, loofah or exfoliating gloves.

03
Step 03

Wait Time

Once the treated hair has shed, your skin may appear smoother for a week or two. New hair growth will gradually appear as follicles enter a new growth phase. Your next session is generally scheduled around 4–6 weeks later, depending on the treatment area.

04
Step 04

Treat Again

After approximately 4–6 weeks, a new cycle of hair growth may become visible. Your next treatment targets these newly active follicles. The process is repeated over multiple sessions according to individual factors and treatment goals.

Please note: Treatment response and the number of sessions can vary from person to person. Your dermatologist or trained laser professional can recommend a schedule based on your skin, hair and treatment area.

What to avoid at the moment

Kin cases me pehle professional advice zaroori hai

  • Sun exposure
    I know this is difficult especially at this time but you really need to be diligent with this. Especially if you are having your facial area treated and are out walking every day. Please remember to apply SPF50 if you are going out. Apply this approx 30 minutes before you are due to leave the house so that it is effective. Be careful if you are sitting in the garden or out on a balcony in the sun to make sure all of your treatment areas are covered. The last thing we want to do is to have to cancel your treatment due to sun exposure when we return so to avoid this happening you really need to be careful during this time.
  • Avoid plucking, waxing, epilating
    Please avoid using any of these methods to remove your hair during this time. By removing the hair from the follicle you may encourage blood flow to the area which may affect your hair growth cycles. I know its really difficult especially if you have facial hair but please try to stick it out for the next few weeks, it will mean your next session of laser will be more effective if your follicles are full of hair.
  • Topical products
    If you are having your facial area treated be sure not to change your skincare routine too much during this time and be careful not to add any new products containing Retinol, Salicylic Acid or any active ingredient that may cause your skin to rapidly exfoliate. If you have started using any of these products be sure to contact us to let us know and stop using them 2 weeks before your next session is due.
  • Medications
    If you changed or started any new medications since your last appointment please send us an email to let us know. We need to run a check over any new medications you may be taking just to make sure they do not cause photosensitivity (sensitive to light).

Important: Treatment suitability depends on individual skin condition and medical history. If you have an active skin problem, allergy, ongoing medication or a recent cosmetic procedure, consult a qualified clinician before treatment.
