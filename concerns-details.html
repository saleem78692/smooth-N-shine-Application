About The Concern

Understanding Acne

Acne-prone skin can often appear congested, uneven and uncomfortable due to excess oil, buildup and clogged pores. A consistent skincare routine combined with professional cleansing can help keep the skin feeling cleaner and fresher.

The Acne Cleanup Facial is designed around gentle cleansing, exfoliation and skin-purifying care to refresh the appearance of acne-prone skin.

Treatment intensity and suitability may vary depending on individual skin condition. Our professionals assess your skin before proceeding with the treatment.
