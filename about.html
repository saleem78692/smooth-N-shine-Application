Before & After Care Protocols

Maximizing safety, comfort, and radiant results

Pre-Treatment Guidelines
  • Avoid direct harsh sun tanning or bleaching for 48 hours prior.
  • Do not wax or thread the targeted laser area before laser sessions.
  • Discontinue heavy AHA/BHA exfoliants 2 days before chemical peels.
  • Keep your skin well-hydrated by drinking ample water.
Post-Treatment Guidelines
  • Apply broad spectrum SPF 50+ sunscreen every 3 to 4 hours.
  • Use gentle hydrating cleanser and soothing aloe/ceramide moisturizer.
  • Avoid steam, sauna, hot showers, and strenuous sweating for 24 hours.
  • Do not scratch, pick, or forcefully exfoliate treated areas.
